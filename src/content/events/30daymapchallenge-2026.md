The [#30DayMapChallenge](https://30daymapchallenge.com/) is a daily mapping challenge open
to everyone. Every November, participants create one map a day for 30 days, each based on
a different theme, and share them on social media using the hashtag **#30DayMapChallenge**.

This year OSGeo:UK is organising maps together in the
[osgeouk/30DayMapChallenge](https://github.com/osgeouk/30DayMapChallenge) repository.
Everyone is welcome, whether you are a GIS expert or have never made a map before.

## Taking part

- Maps can be prepared in advance, but the spirit of the challenge is to publish one on
  each themed day.
- Post your map to Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn, Instagram, X or any platform you prefer
  with the hashtag. No sign-up is needed.
- There are no restrictions on the tools, data or technologies you use, and fewer than 30
  maps is also fine.
- For data sources, software and tutorials, see the official
  [Mapping resources](https://30daymapchallenge.com/resources/) page.

## Collaborating with OSGeo:UK

Each daily theme has its own
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/osgeouk/30DayMapChallenge/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3A2026),
labelled by year.

1. Pick a day and comment on its issue with ideas, data sources and approaches.
2. Assign yourself if you want to take the lead on a map, or join someone else's.
3. Link or attach the finished map to the issue before publishing it on the themed day.

## Code of Conduct

Please follow the challenge's
[Code of Conduct](https://30daymapchallenge.com/#code-of-conduct):

- All maps you publish have to be your original work.
- Give credit to the original data source whenever possible.
- The challenge is about creativity, openness and the joy of beautiful maps and
  cartography. It is not a competition.
- Be smart with AI tools, and be open about how you made your map. Day 10 (Prompting only)
  is the exception for prompt-only maps.
- Don't harass or steal from others.

Follow the hashtag on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/hashtag/30DayMapChallenge),
