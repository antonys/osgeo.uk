Join us for the first ever Geomob Bristol, organised in collaboration with
[OSGeo:UK](https://osgeo.uk/) and the team behind
[FOSS4G 2027](https://2027.foss4g.org/). The evening will bring together the local
geospatial community for short talks and informal networking. The event will also be
streamed online for free as a trial ahead of FOSS4G 2027 in Bristol.

## When and where

**Wednesday 24 February 2027, from 6:00pm** at
[The Robin Hood Pub](https://robinhoodbristol.pub/), 56 St Michael's Hill, Bristol,
BS2 8DX. Find the venue on
[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=51.458630&mlon=-2.599352#map=19/51.458630/-2.599352)
or [Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/n6qz818dyzaePdVK6).

The event will also be streamed online at no cost. Please
[sign up to attend](https://tally.so/r/68bDWO) for the event details.

## Evening schedule

- 6:00pm: Doors open, setup and informal mingling.
- 6:30pm: Welcome and the first talks.
- Talks will run for 10-15 minutes, followed by time for questions.
- Afterwards, continue the conversation downstairs at the pub.

Speakers will be announced soon.

## Accessibility

The event is in the upstairs function room, which is not wheelchair accessible. The pub
itself is accessible.

For full details and updates, see the
[Geomob event announcement](https://thegeomob.com/post/february-24th-2027-geomobbrs-details).
